How Long Does Dental Implant Treatment Take?

Dr. Tahir KORKMAZ

The healthcare field continues to keep pace with the developing world. Solutions are being sought for various diseases through various treatments. There are also different treatment methods in dental treatment. When teeth are completely lost or a few are lost, solutions that can replace the teeth are being developed. In dental treatment, only the teeth are not considered.

Damage can occur in the jaw bones due to lost teeth or any disease. Jaw problems can also occur when all teeth are completely extracted due to aging. The closest method to replace lost teeth is the implant treatment method.

Process of the Implant Method to Replace Lost Teeth

The implant treatment method first begins by examining the jaw structure. It is determined whether the jaw structure to be treated is suitable for implant treatment or not. If the jaw structure is not suitable, the first procedure will be to place an artificial prosthesis in the jaw. The artificial prosthesis that integrates with the jaw bone will create a jaw ready for implant treatment. The implant treatment method is the closest method to reality.

The waiting period for the artificial prosthesis placement process begins. This process varies from person to person. The implant treatment begins once integration with the jaw is achieved. This process is short in individuals with wide jaw structure, while it can take hours in people with narrow jaw structure. Afterwards, the jaw structure getting into order, teeth settling into their places, and trouble-free use cover a time period ranging from 3 to 9 months.

What Are the Stages of Implant Treatment ?

There is a treatment process that varies depending on the patient's condition. It also shows variations depending on the jaw structure. Unless there are very unusual situations, the methods to be applied in the treatment are the same. First, factors such as the suitability of the jaw structure and tooth size are considered by the applied doctor.

For these, dental X-rays are taken. Based on the results from this X-ray, the dentist decides whether a prosthesis is necessary according to the suitability of the jaw structure. Then the size of the tooth to be placed is determined. Methods for implant treatment are determined. If the jaw structure is not suitable, the jaw structure is first made suitable. This procedure can yield results after a waiting period of months. The treatment process fully begins after this waiting period.

Another stage is the placement of the prosthesis and its integration with the jaw. After this procedure is completed, the implant treatment is considered to have fully started and the teeth are placed in their positions. The process following the implant procedure, which depends on the patient's condition, determines the waiting period. The more careful you are, the faster this process will pass. The process that can extend from 6 weeks to 6 months depends entirely on the patient. The prosthesis serving as a jaw and the teeth taking over the function of real teeth will take some time.

It is a process that requires some patience. It is beneficial to be under physician supervision during the treatment process in line with the dentist's recommendation. The doctor may increase or decrease the controls if deemed unnecessary. X-rays may be taken if necessary. Since the jaw structure and tooth roots undergo a procedure requiring close follow-up, it is beneficial to be under doctor supervision during the treatment process.

How Should Implant Care Be During the Treatment Process?

Implant treatment is a method to be applied as a result of the person losing all or some of their teeth. The method that provides the closest teeth to real teeth is the procedure of placing teeth in the jaw. These are time-consuming and long-lasting procedures. It is a process that can extend up to nine months, varying from person to person. The patient may have difficulties during this process.

Therefore, this process depends entirely on the patient. They should be more careful during the treatment process. They should stay away from foods that could damage the teeth. By doing what the doctor recommends, they can shorten this period. The person can shorten the dental treatment process and achieve near-real teeth in a short time. The patient should avoid impacts that could damage the jaw area during and after treatment.

They should not consume hard and shelled foods. The patient should not consume extremely cold and extremely hot products during the implant treatment period. By paying attention to such things, the treatment period is shortened. The patient regains their health in a shorter time and can eat and drink comfortably.

What is the Level of Pain Felt During the Implant Treatment Process ?

Although short-term pains are felt during treatment, these pains disappear after treatment. These pains are due to surgical procedures. They are similar to pains felt in any dental treatment. Short-term problems such as numbness and swelling may be encountered. However, pains that may occur afterwards are pains that can be treated with painkillers.
