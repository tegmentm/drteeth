How to Care for Porcelain Teeth?

Dt TAHİR KORKMAZ

There is no difference between porcelain teeth care and regular oral-dental health care practices. There are only a few separate issues that require more attention. Whether you have porcelain teeth or not, you should brush your teeth two or three times a day. However, you should stay away from the wrong brush choice that many individuals make. You should get help from your dentist to determine the best brush suitable for your general oral-dental health, gum health, tooth structure, etc. In this regard, many of our patients make the mistake of buying and using the first toothbrush they come across. We can continue to explain the toothbrush and other factors regarding proper porcelain teeth care by summarizing them in the subheadings listed below.

Toothbrush in Porcelain Teeth Care

Whether porcelain or natural, you should get support from your dentist to determine the most suitable toothbrush for you. Using the right toothbrush is very important for healthy porcelain teeth care - oral care. You should brush your teeth two to three times a day. Do not interrupt the routine of tooth brushing for proper oral-dental health. You should spend about two minutes brushing your teeth. If you have eaten or drunk before brushing, brush after waiting at least half an hour.

Toothpaste in Porcelain Teeth Care

Do not forget to get the most suitable toothpaste recommendation from your dentist. Stay away from all other toothpastes, tooth powders, etc. products other than the recommended toothpaste. Do not use products with hearsay information for healthy oral-dental health.

Dental Floss in Porcelain Teeth Care

Dental floss is one of the basic care products that all individuals should use, whether natural or porcelain. You will be informed by your dentist about using dental floss. Necessary guidance will also be provided on how to use it. Do not neglect the use of dental floss for maximum oral-dental health.

Mouthwash in Porcelain Teeth Care

Mouthwashes are very valuable products for both natural teeth, porcelain teeth, and oral health-care. You should continue to use mouthwash at least once a day without neglecting its use. Mouthwashes, which are supporters of oral hygiene, have many positive effects, from preventing bad breath, preventing tooth decay, increasing oral care and protection, to supporting the existing whiteness of both porcelain and natural teeth.

Night Guard in Porcelain Teeth Care

Your dentist has already informed you before the porcelain tooth application. Porcelain teeth are not suitable for individuals who have habits such as teeth grinding - teeth clenching at night. However, if such harmful habits of yours are at a low level, porcelain tooth treatment may have been deemed appropriate by your doctor. If you have teeth grinding - teeth clenching, you need to use a night guard before sleeping at night for the health of both your porcelain teeth and your natural teeth. Otherwise, you may not get sufficient and long-term efficiency from your porcelain teeth.

Other Information About Porcelain Teeth Care

  • Avoid eating - breaking hard-shelled foods on front porcelain teeth.
  • Do not apply excessive pressure while brushing.
  • Use mouthwashes. This way you can also support the preservation of your teeth's whiteness.
  • Do not eat or drink anything (until the next meal) after brushing your teeth except water.
  • Do not brush your teeth directly after eating - after any drink. Wait half an hour to brush your teeth. The acids in foods soften the tooth enamel during eating - drinking, and if brushed directly, the tooth surface is damaged.
