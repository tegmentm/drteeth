How Long Does Teeth Whitening Last?

Dr TAHIR KORKMAZ

How long teeth whitening procedures can last depends entirely on you. Under normal conditions, the effect of teeth whitening procedures lasts at least two years. The shortening or extension of this period depends on your oral and dental care. As you know, there are many reasons for teeth discoloration. Even the water we drink can cause teeth to change color. For example, the fluoride level in water can gradually cause tooth color changes over time. One of the biggest factors we can list is cigarettes and other tobacco products. The most teeth whitening requests come from individuals who smoke. If you continue to smoke as before and, worse, continue to perform incorrect - inadequate oral and dental care as before after undergoing teeth whitening stages, you can significantly shorten the longevity of teeth whitening.

How Long Does Teeth Whitening Last for Smokers?

We would like to importantly remind you that if smoking, which is among the top causes of color changes in tooth enamel and even dentin layers, continues after teeth whitening procedures, you may continue to experience color changes again within at most a few years. If you perform proper and timely oral - dental care, you can certainly extend this period. However, if you neglect your oral and dental care, perform incorrect or incomplete practices, you cannot prevent your whitened teeth from changing color again.

How Long Does Teeth Whitening Last According to Age?

As you know, another reason for teeth discoloration is aging. Due to advancing age, the rate of components that should reach the teeth may decrease. Additionally, our teeth that have served us for years may experience deformations due to use. For these and similar reasons, teeth discoloration due to aging is likely. Teeth whitening service is not permanent. Promotions conducted under the name of permanent teeth whitening service are incorrect. How long your teeth will remain white after whitening depends on you. If you are careful after undergoing teeth whitening stages, that is, if you listen to your dentist's recommendations, your effective whiteness rate will continue for years.

How Long Does Teeth Whitening Last for Alcohol Users?

Alcohol is one of the substances harmful to general human health. The first point it damages is oral and dental health. The more frequently and intensely you consume alcohol, the more frequently and intensely your teeth will be damaged. If your frequent and intense alcohol consumption habits continue after teeth whitening procedures, you cannot obtain long-term efficiency from the procedures performed. Additionally, as an alcohol consumer, you should pay more attention to your oral - dental health. Even if you use alcohol, with proper oral - dental care, you can maintain the years-long high-capacity whiteness of teeth whitening.

How Long Does Teeth Whitening Last According to Prosthesis, Coating, Filling?

Procedures such as fillings, root canals, and coatings are performed to ensure that dental health and functionality continue for as many years as possible. You should know that the more you have undergone these procedures, the shorter the effect mechanism may be after teeth whitening procedures. Of course, with regular oral dental care, you can benefit from permanent whiteness with maximum performance. However, you should know that materials such as prosthesis, coating, and filling cannot maintain the general structure of natural teeth. In short, while natural teeth change color over a much longer period, the color-changing lifespan of artificially produced materials such as fillings, coatings, and prostheses may be shorter.

How Long Does Teeth Whitening Last According to Food and Drinks Consumed?

We need to address this topic that many of our patients are unaware of, don't know, or know incorrectly. All the foods we eat and drinks we consume contain acids. Acids can threaten oral - dental health. Of course, if you perform routine teeth cleaning procedures on time, it is possible to protect yourself from these harmful substances. For example, after eating and drinking food, you need to wait about half an hour to brush your teeth. Because the acids in the foods and drinks consumed soften the tooth enamel in dimensions you won't notice. Additionally, the already softened tooth enamel rubs against foods during eating and suffers damage in dimensions you won't notice. Therefore, after solid or liquid nutrition, you should wait half an hour and then brush your teeth. If you don't brush your teeth, wear caused by acid and erosion, both from what you eat at that moment and from foods you will consume later, and color changes arising from the color of foods will also be reflected in your tooth tone.

In summary, teeth whitening methods can offer marvelous levels of tooth tone lightening. However, how long teeth will maintain permanent whiteness depends on the attitude you will display. If you regularly perform your oral - dental care and cleaning, if you consume less tobacco - coffee - tea - alcohol and colored drinks, if you use various medications, especially some antibiotics, as little as possible, if the applications such as fillings - prostheses on your teeth are as few as possible, the teeth whitening effect will last that long. When you listen to your dentists' recommendations, we can comfortably state this; after teeth whitening procedures, you can obtain maximum efficiency from your whitened teeth for at least 2 - 3 years. Then, depending on the advancing year and the listed effects, you may gradually experience changes in your tooth tone, this is a completely natural process.
