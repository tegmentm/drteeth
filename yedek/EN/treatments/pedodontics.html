Pedodontics

Pedodontics is a medical branch that deals with children's teeth. This medical discipline was created to monitor and treat when necessary the baby teeth and permanent teeth of children between 0 and 13 years old. Children may have dental problems due to trauma or cavities. Additionally, teeth can be damaged due to hereditary reasons or external factors. Pedodontics doctors are responsible for eliminating these damages.

Age-Appropriate Treatment Practice in a Peaceful Environment

Pedodontics doctors sometimes take preventive measures to protect the dental health of children and youth. Pedodontics doctors, who perform necessary preventive measures and treatment methods for oral and dental health, carry out what is needed for a healthier oral structure.

What Do Pedodontics Specialists Do?

Pedodontics specialists generally deal with children's dental health. Additionally, they are involved in dental prostheses, tooth extraction, cavity prevention, diagnosis and treatment of dental trauma, root canal treatments, and correction of dental disorders. Pedodontics doctors play important roles in eliminating bad oral habits.

Pedodontics doctors are very important for children's dental development. Therefore, dentists specialize in this field to provide better service. Pedodontics specialists, who provide all kinds of services related to dental structure, are also involved in dental treatments for teenagers. Many different pediatric dental treatment procedures such as treatment and monitoring of baby teeth in children are performed by pedodontics specialists.

Pedodontics and Baby Teeth

When it comes to pedodontics and teeth, baby teeth become important. Baby teeth refer to teeth that appear in early childhood and fall out as age progresses. Baby teeth fall out and are replaced by permanent teeth. A total of 20 baby teeth emerge, and these teeth need to be extracted over time. Procedures such as monitoring these teeth and extracting them when necessary are performed by pedodontics specialists.

Necessary monitoring and treatments for not extracting baby teeth prematurely and ensuring children's baby teeth are extracted on time are carried out with pedodontics specialists. Therefore, it's beneficial to visit a pedodontics specialist for regular dental monitoring from early ages to advanced ages. Pedodontics specialists play roles for reasons such as complete dental development.

Dental Development in Children

Dental development in children begins from the infancy stage. Dental development in babies begins between 6 and 12 months when the first teeth and baby teeth start to emerge. Completion of baby teeth in children continues until age 3. A total of 20 of these so-called baby teeth are expected. These baby teeth continue their functions until age 6 or 7.

It's inevitable that parents don't have sufficient knowledge about dental development during both infancy and childhood. Therefore, it would be appropriate to perform dental monitoring with expert help from infancy to the end of childhood. Healthy teeth and oral structure significantly affect dental development in later stages.

Pedodontics and Preventive Dentistry

Pediatric dentistry, as it's commonly known, plays an important role in dental development with its preventive function. Preventive measures are implemented by dentists both for guiding parents and for children to acquire habits related to oral health. Preventive dental measures are implemented through fluoride and fissure sealant applications.

The progression of dental cavities occurs as bacteria fill the cavities that form on tooth surfaces over time. Therefore, fissure sealants prevent the formation of dental cavities. In addition to fissures, preventive measures are also implemented with fluoride application. Since tooth enamel is unprotected in early childhood stages, fluoride supplementation prevents tooth decay.

Pedodontology and Dental Examination

To protect children's dental health, teeth need to be regularly monitored. Therefore, regular monitoring with a pedodontics specialist, meaning a pediatric dentist, is necessary. For this purpose, check-ups can begin with children aged 6 months or 1 year. For such reasons, parents regularly monitor to address dental problems at an early stage.

Pedodontics specialists regularly monitor children's dental development, detect deteriorations in dental structure in time, and perform necessary interventions. When necessary, they apply various treatments including braces to correct dental disorders in children. Regular monitoring of children's oral health with a pedodontist is very important for dental development.

Why Is a Pedodontist Needed?

Children's development is different from adults. Therefore, a different specialty is needed for children's dental and oral health. Pedodontics is a discipline that emerged for this reason. Childhood period shows differences from adulthood in terms of development. Therefore, procedures specific to this period need to be performed.

Children expend more effort in terms of metabolism and development during this period. Additionally, teeth in this period are less durable compared to adult teeth. Due to these differences, a different medical field needed to be created. Therefore, pedodontics specialists are needed for children's dental health.

Why Is Children's Oral and Dental Health Important?

The reason why oral and dental health is important in children is that the mouth is in close relationship with many organs. Therefore, monitoring oral and dental health is important. With early detection of crookedness seen in some children's teeth, necessary interventions can be performed. Additionally, some responsibilities fall on dentists regarding oral health.

First of all, bad breath or oral health seen in children may be related to teeth or may indicate other problems. Therefore, regular monitoring of oral and dental health and the role that falls to dentists in this regard are of great importance.

Benefits of Regular Dental Monitoring

There are many benefits to regular dental monitoring in children. Among these benefits, protecting children's jaw structure can be mentioned first. Following this, benefits such as proper progression of dental development, elimination of oral problems, and absence of dental cavities are achieved during regular monitoring.

Additionally, since childhood is a period where development is most important and fastest, teeth develop very quickly during this period. For such reasons, regular dental monitoring is necessary to prevent developmental disorders in teeth during this rapid period. It's seen that this pedodontics service is provided in many of our cities. There are many health centers providing services in the field of pedodontics.

Recommendations for Children's Dental Health

There are various recommendations for children's dental health. First of all, knowing that there are different periods for dental health is beneficial. Dental development in children is evaluated separately between 0-6 years, separately between 6-12 years, and separately at 12 years and above. The period between 0-6 years is called the baby teeth period, between 6-12 years is the mixed dentition period, and above 12 years is the permanent dentition period.

It's beneficial to know that different factors come to the forefront regarding children's dental development in each period. Therefore, knowing these dental periods and receiving services from a specialist when necessary for dental monitoring is very beneficial. Each period has its own characteristics.

Common Dental Health Problems in Children

There are a series of problems related to dental development in children. The first is baby teeth not developing as desired. In this disorder seen in early childhood, deficiencies or disorders can be seen in children's teeth. Another dental problem is bottle cavities - these problems seen due to children's constant bottle use become apparent at an early stage.

Another dental problem seen in children is problems caused by thumb sucking. This problem, usually seen in individuals under 5 years old, can cause deteriorations in tooth structure. Additionally, dental problems arise due to children consuming too much sugar and similar products.

Solutions for Dental Problems in Children

Pedodontics specialists apply various treatments to resolve dental problems seen in children. Filling treatment is a treatment method applied to fill tooth spaces in children as well as in adults. In this treatment, tooth development is achieved by applying fillings to tooth cavities. Additionally, with space maintainers, it's possible to fill spaces left empty after tooth loss.

Another treatment method used to overcome dental problems in children is root canal treatment. Root canal treatment is applied to replace teeth. Previously, inflammatory structures in canal areas are eliminated with antibiotics. Tooth extraction can also be applied when necessary.

At the same time, extraction of dental cavities and implementation of measures related to oral health are among the services provided by pedodontics specialists in health centers. It should be stated that these services are very important for children's development and also have benefits regarding jaw structure. Children's and dental health are regularly monitored by pedodontics specialists, and oral and jaw structure issues can be eliminated.
